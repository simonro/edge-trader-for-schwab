# Code of Conduct

Edge Trader for Schwab is a small project built in the open. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented, is expected to keep it a place
where people can ask questions and disagree without being attacked.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to code or to trading.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including their data or account details.
- Spam, promotion of paid services, and unsolicited financial advice.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, message [@tapetoedge on X](https://x.com/tapetoedge)
or use GitHub's private reporting on the Security tab. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
